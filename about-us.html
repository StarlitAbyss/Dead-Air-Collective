Dead Air Collective

Our Mission is Simple

Born from a group of friends who wanted to support each other, Dead Air Collective exists solely to support Creators. We do not own our Talent's Intellectual Property nor do we take revenue cuts.

What we Offer

Providing Management, live2d models, assets, and marketing, as well as organizing both creator focused and cross-community projects - within our means. We hope to be able to do much more in the future.

Members also naturally are the priority of and enjoy special treatment from our merchandising branch.

Our Values

We like to be open about our inner workings. We do not support idol culture and instead focus on empowering creators to express themselves authentically.
